Bookmarks bar separator

A thin gray line to keep your Chrome bookmarks in groups.

WorkPersonal
  1. Bookmark this page with ⌘D on Mac or Ctrl+D on Windows and Linux.
  2. Choose Bookmarks bar. Leave the name blank, or clear any visible text, then save.
  3. Drag the new separator between your bookmark groups.

Need more? Copy and paste this bookmark in Chrome’s bookmark manager.
